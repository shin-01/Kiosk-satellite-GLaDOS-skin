# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial release.

- GLaDOS voice skin: Aperture eye that flares with the voice and the answer, HUD frame with bracket corners, scan lines and tick marks, GLaDOS-flavored status captions.
- Full screen and docked looks, following Voice Satellite's Theme, Background, Text size and Reactive activity bar settings.

## 0.2.0

- Iconic Portal lines in English rotate on the caption line while the skin waits: "THE CAKE IS A LIE.", "STILL ALIVE.", "WE DO WHAT WE MUST BECAUSE WE CAN.", "THE ENRICHMENT CENTER IS MANDATORY.", "SCIENCE ISN'T ABOUT WHY, IT'S ABOUT WHY NOT.", "THIS WAS A TRIUMPH."
- One line per turn: each new wake word brings the next.

## 0.3.0

- Twelve iconic Portal lines in rotation, one every 7 seconds while the skin waits, plus one per turn.
- More GLaDOS-like status captions: "I'M LISTENING. TRY NOT TO DISAPPOINT ME.", "THINKING. FOR BOTH OF US.", "RESPONDING. NOTE THE SCIENCE.", "RESPONSE COMPLETE. YOU'RE WELCOME."

## 0.4.0

- GLaDOS in potato form, from Portal's second act: lumpy brown potato body with baked spots, the Aperture eye jammed in with a metal bolted ring, a glowing yellow lens with a red pupil, and clipped wires swaying from the back.
- Simpler shapes than the full eye, cheaper to render on weak kiosks.

## 0.5.0

- The potato is now a static photorealistic picture shipped in the plugin's assets; only the eye's light animates over it: the glow that flares with the voice and the answer, and the breathing red pupil.
- Lighter still: one bitmap draw plus a few circles per frame.
