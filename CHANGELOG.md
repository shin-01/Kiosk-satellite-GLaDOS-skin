# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial release.

- GLaDOS voice skin: Aperture eye that flares with the voice and the answer, HUD frame with bracket corners, scan lines and tick marks, GLaDOS-flavored status captions.
- Full screen and docked looks, following Voice Satellite's Theme, Background, Text size and Reactive activity bar settings.
